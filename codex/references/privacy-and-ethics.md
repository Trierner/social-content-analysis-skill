# Privacy And Ethics

Use this reference for safety boundaries.

## Do Not Do

- Do not bypass logins, paywalls, private profiles, deleted content, or platform restrictions.
- Do not deanonymize users.
- Do not infer sensitive attributes about private people.
- Do not help harass, shame, or target individuals.
- Do not compile personal dossiers.
- Do not present rumors as facts.
- Do not fabricate metrics, dates, quotes, comments, or creator intent.

## Private Or Sensitive Content

If the user provides private content they own or are allowed to share:

- analyze only what is needed
- avoid exposing unnecessary personal data
- summarize rather than reproduce private messages
- anonymize names when useful
- ask before using it as source material for public-facing output

## Public Figures And Brands

Public brands, creators, and public figures can be analyzed based on public content, but:

- distinguish criticism of content from personal attacks
- avoid unsupported claims about motives or private facts
- use evidence-based language

## Children, Health, Finance, Legal, Politics

Use extra caution for content involving:

- minors
- health or mental health
- financial advice
- legal claims
- political persuasion
- identity or protected traits

Prefer explanation, risk analysis, and neutral summarization over manipulation.

## Originality

When generating ideas from others' content:

- adapt patterns, do not copy wording
- credit source patterns when relevant
- create new examples, claims, and proof
- avoid imitating a creator's distinctive voice too closely
